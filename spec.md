2. LinkGuard — “Is this link safe?” 
Problem Statement
People frequently receive unknown or suspicious links through social media, messaging apps, SMS, and email. Many users cannot determine whether a link is legitimate or potentially dangerous before opening it. Malicious links can lead to phishing websites, malware, fake offers, or stolen personal information. Therefore, there is a need for a simple tool that helps users check links before clicking them.

Objective
The objective of LinkGuard is to develop a simple cybersecurity tool that allows users to check suspicious links before opening them. The system will analyze factors such as domain reputation, HTTPS usage, URL structure, redirects, and known malicious indicators. It will provide a clear risk level and explanation to help users make safer decisions when accessing online links.
